Academic Coordinator Tools — Privacy

Last updated 22 August 2026

Academic Coordinator Tools is a private administrative application maintained by the academic coordinator for Sciences & Engineering (SCEN) at Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi. It is used by that department's staff to prepare syllabi, keep part-time teacher records, and publish student timetables. It is not a public service and has no accounts other than the staff invited to it.

What signing in gives us

Signing in uses your Google account. Google tells the application your e-mail address and your display name; it never gives us your password. Those two details are used to check that you are on the staff list, to show who is signed in, and to record when you last signed in. A signed cookie keeps you signed in for twelve hours; clearing it or signing out ends the session.

What the application stores

  • The staff list: e-mail address, display name, whether the account administers the application, and when it was invited and last used.
  • The academic records staff enter or upload: course syllabi, part-time teacher profiles and recruitment paperwork, and semester timetables with the course registration numbers each student is enrolled in.

Student timetable data identifies students by their university ID and holds no names or contact details.

What we do not do

There is no advertising, no analytics or tracking of visitors, and nothing here is sold, rented, or shared with anyone outside the department. Data is not used to train anything.

Where it is held

The application and its PostgreSQL database run on hosted infrastructure (FastAPI Cloud) on the department's behalf, and are reachable only over HTTPS.

Removing your access or your data

An administrator can remove a staff account at any time from the application's own settings, which ends that person's access immediately. To ask what is held about you, or to have it deleted, write to christian.kha.work@gmail.com.
