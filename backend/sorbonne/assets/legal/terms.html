Academic Coordinator Tools — Terms of use

Last updated 22 August 2026

Academic Coordinator Tools is a private administrative application maintained by the academic coordinator for Sciences & Engineering (SCEN) at Sorbonne University Abu Dhabi, for the use of that department's staff.

Who may use it

Only accounts on the application's staff list may sign in. Access is granted, suspended, and withdrawn by an administrator, at any time and without notice. Do not share your session or sign in on behalf of somebody else.

How it may be used

Use it for the academic administration it was built for. The records it holds concern colleagues and students, so treat what you read here as confidential and follow the university's own rules on handling that information.

No warranty

The application is provided as it is, for internal convenience. It may change, be interrupted, or be withdrawn without notice, and it is not a system of record for the university: keep the official records where the university keeps them.
