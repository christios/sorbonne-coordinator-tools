# Quick wins, September 2026

Confirmed intent, 13 September 2026. Project 3 of three, built first.

- **Escape in a search box** drops focus and keeps the text. A second Escape does nothing.
- **Expected pill on group cards** goes. Seats read like "36 / 40" with the small
  expected mark inline, the expected number in its tooltip.
- **Filters offer every column,** hidden ones marked. Picking a hidden column adds the
  filter and shows the column at the end; it stays shown after the filter is removed.
- **Every filter field is a dropdown** of the column's values, with a switch to free
  text when needed. Check every table.
- **Active teachers gets a "Teaches" column** listing the component types they are
  named on in the planning: CM, TD, TP.
- **A CRN record lists its registered students:** name (from this browser), cohort and
  the group of ours that stands for this CRN or "no group of theirs"; each line opens
  the student record.
- **The arrivals banner** on Cohorts gets "Add to this cohort" per line, with the same
  confirmation as the Move to cohort dialog.
- **Floating bar placement dropdowns** say "would clash" beside an option that overlaps
  a group the selected students already hold or one just chosen in another set's
  dropdown of the same bar, from the same report the proposal uses.
- **The coordinator calendar** (student, teacher, course and CRN records) shows group
  and CRN in every full-size box, plus a line at the current time on today's column.
  The student hub's week is unchanged.
- **Parallel groups:** a field on the group card, beside hours and notes, to choose the
  other groups it must be scheduled in parallel with; it travels with the timetable
  request.
- **Source marks on every teacher surface** — the profile, the Teachers table and
  Active teachers — saying what comes from our planning and what from the portal.
- **Signature column** on the students table: one readable token per student such as
  "CM Maths · TD 3 · PHIL-TD 1 · MTP 3A", every set of the semester in a fixed order,
  so filtering finds students who share every class.
- **Out of scope here:** anything that needs sub-rows or cohort rules.
