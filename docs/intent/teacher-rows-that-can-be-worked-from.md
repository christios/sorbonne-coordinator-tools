# Teacher rows you can work from without opening anyone

Confirmed intent, 14 September 2026. About the Part-time Teacher Database's list of
teachers, not the Active teachers page in Students and Timetables.

- **The row is where the work happens.** A row says a name, an email it usually does
  not have, a folder, and "0/0 tasks done". Everything worth knowing about a teacher
  is one click further in, so a sweep across 24 people means opening 24 profiles.
- **What a row says:** how many requisitions and how many time sheets the teacher has,
  whether their documents arrived, the period the newest time sheet covers, and the
  hours their requisitions pay for. A zero or a blank is the gap. That matters because
  there is no one thing that is always the gap: it varies by teacher and by month.
- **What a row does:** open a time sheet, with a choice of which one where there are
  several and the newest offered first; download requisitions, choosing one, some or
  all, arriving as a single zip the way the documents button already works; and start
  a new requisition, landing in its editor.
- **A time sheet gains its period,** and the period is the department's pay cycle: the
  15th of one month to the 14th of the next. It is one choice from that standing cycle
  rather than a pair of months, which is why five of the nine sheets filed for 2026-27
  are named "AugSept" and are one period, not two. Without it a row can only count
  sheets, and cannot say who is overdue.
- **The nine time sheets already on production** were filed before the period existed
  and need one adding.
- **The list stays a list.** Not a sortable table like the Students page, and nothing
  hidden behind a hover: the facts and the buttons are all on show, and a busier page
  is the accepted price.
- **Tasks stay where they are,** behind the derived facts rather than in front of them.
  What the system can work out for itself costs nobody any upkeep and is therefore
  never stale; a task list is for the things it cannot know, and is not being reworked
  here.
- **Success:** you can see who is missing something for the current period without
  opening anyone, and reach a sheet or a contract in one click.
- **Out of scope:** ticking several teachers to move or archive them together;
  archiving from the row; turning the list into a sortable table; a separate view that
  lists only the gaps; and any change to the Active teachers page.
